The Change Curve

Use the audio player below the change curve image to hear about the Kubler-Ross Change Curve. Then, hover over each stage on the curve to learn more about each part of the change process.
Audio Transcript

Change can be tough, and the Kübler-Ross Change Curve helps us understand how people typically react to it. Originally based on the stages of grief, it describes the emotional journey we experience during major change. It starts with Shock and Denial, when we resist or struggle to accept what’s happening. Then we often move into Frustration or Depression, as the reality of the change sets in. Over time, we reach Experimentation—trying new ways of working or thinking. This leads to Acceptance, and finally, Integration, where the change becomes part of our new normal. Understanding these stages helps leaders and teams support one another, building empathy and resilience through times of change.
